Cosmetic Dentistry

Treatments that improve the color, shape and alignment of your teeth for a smile you feel proud of.

Includes

  • Teeth whitening
  • Porcelain veneers
  • Dental bonding
  • Smile makeover planning

Procedure

  1. Smile consultation and discussion of your goals.
  2. Shade matching and treatment preview.
  3. Teeth are cleaned and prepared for the chosen treatment.
  4. Whitening gel, bonding resin or veneers are applied and shaped.
  5. Final polish, bite check and care instructions.
Whitening: about 1 hourVeneers: 2 visitsRecovery: minimal
