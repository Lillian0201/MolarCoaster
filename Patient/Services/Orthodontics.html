Orthodontics

Straighten teeth and correct bite problems to improve both appearance and oral health.

Includes

  • Metal and ceramic braces
  • Clear aligners
  • Retainers after treatment
  • Bite and spacing correction

Procedure

  1. Assessment with X-rays, photos and impressions or scans.
  2. Custom treatment plan with estimated timeline.
  3. Braces or aligners are fitted.
  4. Regular adjustment visits, usually every 4–8 weeks.
  5. Retainers are fitted to keep teeth in their new position.
Treatment: 12–24 monthsAdjustments: monthlyRetainer: long-term
