What this treatment involves, step by step

Oral Surgery

Safe, carefully planned surgical care for teeth and tissues that cannot be treated with ordinary procedures.

Includes

  • Tooth extraction, including wisdom teeth
  • Impacted tooth removal
  • Minor soft-tissue surgery
  • Pre-implant bone and gum preparation

Procedure

  1. Consultation, X-rays and a written treatment plan.
  2. Local anesthesia so the area is fully numb.
  3. The tooth or tissue is removed or treated.
  4. Stitches are placed if needed, and gauze controls bleeding.
  5. Aftercare instructions and medication are given; a follow-up is scheduled.
Duration: 30–90 minutesRecovery: 3–7 daysFollow-up: 1 week
