Restorative Dentistry

Repair or replace damaged and missing teeth to bring back comfortable chewing, speech and confidence.

Includes

  • Fillings and inlays
  • Crowns and bridges
  • Dentures (partial and complete)
  • Root canal treatment and dental implants

Procedure

  1. Examination and X-rays to assess the damage.
  2. Treatment options and costs explained before starting.
  3. Area is numbed; decay is removed and the tooth is shaped.
  4. Filling, crown, bridge or implant is placed or an impression is taken.
  5. Final fitting, bite adjustment and aftercare guidance.
Fillings: 1 visitCrowns/bridges: 2 visitsImplants: several months
