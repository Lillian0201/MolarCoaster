General Dentistry

Routine care that keeps your teeth and gums healthy and catches problems early, before they become painful or costly.

Includes

  • Oral examination and X-rays
  • Professional cleaning (oral prophylaxis)
  • Fluoride treatment and dental sealants
  • Simple tooth fillings

Procedure

  1. Review of your dental and medical history.
  2. Visual examination, with X-rays when needed.
  3. Scaling and polishing to remove plaque and tartar.
  4. Fluoride or sealant application if recommended.
  5. Personalised home-care advice and next-visit schedule.
Duration: 30–60 minutesRecommended: every 6 monthsRecovery: none
