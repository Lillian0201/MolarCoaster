# MolarCoaster Dental Clinic Website

## Project Overview
This website was developed by BSIS 3rd year students as part of their Web Development course. It showcase the appointment booking, clinic profile, dental services, patient resources, and contact information of MolarCoaster Dental Clinic, a fictional dental clinic that provides accessible, reliable, and quality dental care for individuals and families of all ages.

## Purpose
The purpose of the MolarCoaster Dental Clinic website is to provide an accessible and user-friendly online platform where patients can easily learn about the clinic, explore available dental services, and access essential information about appointments, clinic hours, and contact details.

## Design Concept
- **Color Scheme** - Soft blue and white as the primary colors, with light yellow, pastel orange, or soft green accents for a clean, calming, and playful dental theme.

- **Typography** - Clean and readable fonts, with distinctive headings and simple body text for better readability.

- **Layout** - Consistent spacing, organized sections, clear navigation, and a responsive layout for different devices.
